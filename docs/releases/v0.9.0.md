## What's new in 0.9.0

- **Knowledge, in one place.** One view (⌘6) for everything your agents know: your business, your documents, their memory, their skills and training. Skills, Memory and Learn now live there, and every old link and pin lands in the right place.
- **Documents your agents read.** Drop in a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, Markdown or text file (up to 30 MB, teammates too). McBot keeps the original, turns it into text, and every agent that uses skills can read it when the work calls for it.
- **Export your knowledge as Markdown.** Your business, documents, memory and skills, in one `.md` file you can read, share or give to any AI.
- **Training that runs.** Train a model of your own on your agents' best conversations, right in McBot. In the cloud: through your provider's fine-tuning API, with the key you already saved, and McBot follows the job. On this computer: a Trainer agent fine-tunes an open model with Unsloth and serves it with llama.cpp, and your data never leaves. **Use it** makes the new model the agent's own and keeps the old one as its backup.
- **Tasks keep going until done.** New tasks run until a check on the agent's own model agrees the work is finished (up to 20 rounds), then wait for your review. **Mark done** always works for a person, with or without words.
- **Agents ask before they guess.** When something is unclear, an agent asks you in the chat, with choices, several at once, or room for your own words, and carries on with your answer. Questions also show in Home's Needs you and the island.
- **Guardrails per agent.** Choose how careful each agent is (Always ask, Smart, Never ask), the commands it may never run (held even when it never asks), and the websites it may not open. They sit in Layers → Guardrails and on the agent's page, and the Share sheet warns when a guest could reach an agent that never asks.
- **Chat knows the agent's tasks.** Every message carries the agent's open tasks, how far each got and what it finished today, so you can ask "how's the launch post going?" and get a real answer. The task panel shows the work so far, live, and says "Looks stuck" after 10 quiet minutes.
- **Agents know where they work.** Every agent knows it runs in McBot Studio, which models it thinks with and where they run, and which tools and makers it has, and can read McBot's own guide when you ask how something works.
- **Team.** See everyone with access and their role, remove anyone, invite from the Team view, and set your name, what you do and your colour.
- **Glass pop-up menus** replace the system's grey dropdowns everywhere, and agents show their face in them.
- **A calmer Home** with one job: ask, see your team, see what needs you.
- **No two agents share a name.** A second Writer becomes "Writer 2", and the doctor renames twins a board already has.
- **Star and follow.** Star McBot on GitHub and follow it on X from Settings → About or ⌘K.
- **More logos:** OpenAI, xAI, Krea, Groq, fal, DeepInfra, Together, Canva and DingTalk.
- Fixed: a run could fail with "runtime_draining" while the engine restarted; it now waits and is sent again, and failures read as sentences.
- Fixed: the doctor now warns when a lead hands work to a team on a small local model, which tends to answer by itself.
- Fixed: publishing a release no longer builds a second, stray draft.
