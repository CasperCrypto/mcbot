## What's new in 0.8.0

- **A team that knows your business.** Setup now asks, step by step, what you do, what you sell, who your customers are, your goals, your voice and where you post. Every agent knows it: it goes into each agent's instructions, and you can change it any time in Team knowledge (⌘K).
- **Teams that do the work, not just talk about it.** Pick what you want done (social media, sales, support, research, a launch…) and McBot builds the team with the tools it needs: the designer makes the images itself, the researcher searches the web, the video maker makes the video. Ask McBot's plans and the New agent cards bring their tools too. New: Social media and Sales teams.
- **Images and video with Hugging Face, free.** Setup offers Hugging Face (sign in with your account) as the way your agents make pictures and video, through its Spaces. It is also first in the tool catalog's new Images & video section, next to Comfy Cloud. OpenRouter, OpenAI, FAL and xAI still work with their keys.
- **Learn: train a model of your own on your agents' work.** Rate answers with 👍 / 👎, add checks that grade every answer (mentions, never mentions, used a tool, length, valid JSON), and export an agent's best conversations as training data: OpenAI's fine-tuning format, with scores, or Hermes' own format. Keys and tokens are left out. Links to train on a Mac, on a free GPU or with a hosted service.
- **See the plan.** When an agent breaks a job into steps, its answer shows the checklist live: done, working on, next.
- **A bigger, clearer McBot.** Everything is drawn at 120% by default; Settings → Appearance → Size (or ⌘+ / ⌘−) picks 100–145%.
- **The island, useful.** Wider, and it opens only when you rest on it or click it, never when you pass by. It shows what needs you, what each agent is doing right now, today's spend and runs, what runs next, a box to ask any agent, quick actions, and Minimize / Maximize for the window.
- **Layers you can change.** The board's second view shows one agent's whole setup and lets you change steps per message, helpers, thinking and its daily budget right there. The read-only Hermes map is gone; the Board / Layers switch moved to the top left, clear of the island.
- **Colour that helps you find things.** Brand logos are solid colour tiles you recognise at a glance, block kinds have their colours back, and the board's dock is bigger and colourful again, with icons that grow under the pointer.
- **A calmer look.** No outlines around cards and panels: fills and soft depth instead, frosted glass for what floats, white main buttons, state shown as small muted dots, more room everywhere and less text.
- **More logos.** Exa, Tavily, Firecrawl, Playwright, Slack, Teams, Monday, Amplitude, Close, Comfy Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Learn and Twilio, and clean icons for email, SMS, IRC, knowledge and thinking.
- **More models.** OpenCode Go and Concentrate (one key, 100+ models, automatic fallbacks) are in Settings → Models and setup.
- **Cost knows local models are free.** Runs on a model on your computer show as Free, and McBot no longer asks you to price them.
- **The engine is Hermes v0.23.3**, upstream's newest release.
- Report a problem and exports hide more kinds of tokens (GitHub, Slack, AWS, Google, JWTs).
- Fixed: the island opened when the pointer crossed it to reach the Board / Layers switch, and cut off its content on short windows.
