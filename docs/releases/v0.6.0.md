## What's new in 0.6.0

- **Ask McBot to build your harness.** Describe the agents you need ("a support team that answers customers on Slack from our docs") and McBot proposes a whole team: each agent's job, tools, chat apps, Hermes skills and hand-offs. Review it, then add it to the board; one ⌘Z takes it all back. Your board's own model designs it when one is set up; otherwise McBot plans it from your words. Open it from the board's dock, ⌘K, the empty board or New agent.
- **See every agent in layers.** The board's new Layers tab shows each agent's harness from the outside in: Surfaces, Loop, Context, Model, Tools, Plugins and Guardrails. It says what the board sets, what Hermes decides by default and what's missing, and one click opens the place to change it.
- **Steps per message.** Cap how many tool steps an agent may take on one message (Inspector, or the Loop layer). It goes straight into the agent's Hermes setup.
- After adding a starter team or a plan, the board zooms to show it.
