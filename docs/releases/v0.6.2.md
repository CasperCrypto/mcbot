## What's new in 0.6.2

- **When the engine doesn't start, McBot Studio says why.** Instead of a vague "engine didn't start", the window now tells you what happened: the engine stopped (and how), your antivirus or system blocked it, another program is using McBot's port, or it's still starting. It shows the end of the engine's log, with **Copy details** to paste into an issue and **Restart engine** to try again without quitting the app.
