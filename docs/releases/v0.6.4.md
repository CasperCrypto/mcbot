## What's new in 0.6.4

- **Drawings.** Agents can answer with a drawing (Excalidraw): a flow, a plan, how a system fits together. It shows right in the chat; Open makes it full size to move things, add to it and Save to Files. `.excalidraw` files in Files open the same way and save back. New agents know how to draw.
- **The island shows what went wrong.** Moving to the island no longer hides a compile error: it opens with your recent notices (with Fix or Files) and everything the harness doctor found, each with its fix.
- **Bigger, clearer text** across the app.
- **Sticky notes move.** Drag a note by its top. New notes land in free space instead of under a block.
- Fixed: the agent inspector cut off its fields when a model's name was long.
- Fixed: the board's dock ran under the inspector; it now fits beside it.
