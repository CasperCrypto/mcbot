# Code of conduct

McBot Studio is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want
taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain rather than dismiss.
- Keep criticism about the work, and make it specific and useful.
- Welcome newcomers; everyone's first issue or pull request counts.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## If something happens

Report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability",
which only maintainers see), or to the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile. We will look into every report,
keep it confidential, and act in proportion: from a word in private to removing someone from the project.

This applies in the repository, its issues and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.
